Cues

Your run of show: the cues in the order your service runs. The clock starts at one of them and counts down to the ones after it, each expected when the element before it ends.

Cues

  • Drag to put the cues in service order. With the handle focused, the arrow keys move a cue too.
  • A cue matches when its name contains every word of one of its phrases, in any order, and none of its exclude words. The service prefix (9AM //, 11:15 -) is set aside first.
  • Est. length is how long that element usually runs: minutes (45), m:ss (5:30) or h:mm:ss (1:05:00). Each cue is expected when the one before it ends.
  • A real cue always replaces its estimate. With no cue yet, the clock runs over (−1:05) instead of moving on.
